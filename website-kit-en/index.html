Frequently Asked Questions

Here you will find answers about usage, scope and limitations of the Website Kit.

Do I need coding skills?

No. Basic understanding helps, but the documentation guides you through the setup step by step.

Does it work without WordPress?

Yes. The Website Kit is based on static HTML and CSS. It does not require WordPress or a database.

Can I customize colors and content?

Yes. Colors, copy, images and structure can be customized.

Is hosting included?

No. You need your own hosting and your own domain.

Is support included?

No. This product is intentionally built as a digital self-service kit. Individual support, personal setup and custom requests are not included.

Can I resell the template?

No. Redistribution or resale of the template, individual files or documentation is not allowed.

Is there a German and English version?

Yes. The German and English versions are available separately.
